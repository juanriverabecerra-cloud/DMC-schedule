# DMC Anesthesia Schedule

A phone-first web app for DMC anesthesia residents that combines the three schedules we get:

1. **Annual rotations** (`.xlsx`, one sheet per class): what rotation everyone is on each month.
2. **Monthly call schedule** (`.xlsx`): daily codes and colors (H1, H2, NF, 10-7, late, cardiac call, vacation).
3. **Daily OR sheet** (photo from WhatsApp): room, surgeon, start time, MDA, JA and breaks.
4. **Didactics** (monthly conference-schedule PDF): Wednesday conference, Monday board review, Friday mock orals.

What it shows:

- **Today**: your assignment, OR details, who you're working with, the week, upcoming call, and estimated duty hours, in the order you choose (see Arrange).
- **Tonight**: late resident, CA-3 10a–7p, the CA-1 on 11 to 9 (OB until 9p), night float, 24/12-hour call, cardiac call, JAs, OB and today's attendings, with call/text buttons.
- **Board**: who's where today, grouped by site and OR, with search and class filters.
- **Hours**: estimated weekly hours for everyone against ACGME limits (80 h/4-week average, 1 day off in 7, 24+4, rest between shifts).
- **Gaps**: missing late/NF/24-hr/12-hr/cardiac coverage, the call sheet's own "coverage needed" rows, and conflicts with the daily sheet.
- **Swap finder**: for any call shift, same-class residents who are free and would stay under the hour limits.
- **Fairness (Admin only)**, laid out for the chiefs in five tabs. *Overview*: the year's grades, a **burnout watch** (who has had a heavy last 4 weeks or has a heavy 2 weeks ahead: hours, heavy calls, weekends, days off, days in a row, load against the class, ACGME limits) and a **plan for next month** (each resident's target points, most under their share first, which kinds of shift each is due for or has had plenty of, and who takes no call). *Cover*: the "Someone's out" tool and suggested swaps. *Shifts*: weighted burden points per resident against their class, year by year, services against minimums, and the rotation mix. *Hours*: the real hours residents added, which rotations run longest, and ACGME limits. *Setup*: minimums, the residents' ratings and the points per shift. Balances start over every July. It advises; people decide, and the call sheet stays the source of truth.
- **Someone's out**: the jeopardy resident always comes first. After that, coverage stays within the class, ranked by fewest warnings and then by who is furthest under their fair share. Anyone who would break an ACGME limit is left out, with the reason shown.
- **ACGME limits**: every resident checked against the anesthesiology requirements (sections 6.20 to 6.28): 80 hours averaged over 4 weeks, 14 hours off after 24-hour call, 1 day in 7 off, 24 + 4 continuous hours, and call at most every third night. These are estimates from the schedule, not logged hours.
- **Days off** (More › Days off): golden weekends so far and ahead, and the days you and the people you pick are all off.
- **Exams** (More › Exams): personal countdowns. Everyone gets the ITE; BASIC or ADVANCED defaults by class and each resident can change it, privately, on their own phone. A call sheet that marks someone's exam day wins. Session dates are in `public/core/exams.js`.
- **No-call scorecard** (Admin › Fairness › Shifts): of the days each resident asked for, how many the call sheet honored. It counts only lists sent before that month's sheet was uploaded.
- **Arrange** (More › Arrange, and a link at the bottom of Today): each person picks their tabs (up to four, from Today, Tonight, Board, Didactics, Cases, Call, Hours, Days off, Exams, Swaps and Gaps; Today always stays, More stays last) and their order, and the first tab is the one the app opens on. Today's cards can be moved up or down or hidden; anything waiting on the person (a swap request, a reminder) still comes first, and the day card can't be hidden. Kept on each device (`dmc.tabs`, `dmc.todayLayout`), with cards added in later updates slotted in after the one they follow by default. Logic in `public/core/layout.js`.
- **Every screen shape**: one column on a phone, a side rail when it's turned sideways, two columns on a wide screen such as an unfolded iPhone Duo or a tablet. The two columns are two real stacks, balanced so the taller one is as short as it can be, with a section title kept with the card under it (`splitColumns` in `public/js/ui.js`). A list a screen redraws in place (Board, Didactics, Call) is marked `.flow` and split again after each search or filter, and everything is laid out again when the width changes (folding, turning).
- **iPhone app and widgets** (`native/`): the same app in a native frame, with home-screen and lock-screen widgets and alerts through Apple. See [native/README.md](native/README.md).
- **Demo sign-in**: set `DEMO_PASSCODE` and that passcode opens an invented program, for app reviewers or for showing the app without the real schedule.
- **Rate the shifts**: residents rate how heavy each shift feels (More › Rate the shifts). Ratings are stored apart from the schedule and only shown combined, to admins, once at least 3 people have rated a shift. An admin decides whether to use them as the points.
- **Calendar feed**: subscribe once in Apple or Google Calendar and every shift arrives with its OR details, room phone, MDA's number and who's on.
- **Didactics**: the month's conferences as an agenda, with "Next up", one-tap **Join Zoom** and **Dial in** (it dials, then enters the meeting ID and passcode), filters (Wednesday, Monday, mock orals, mine), objectives and the original PDF. On days with didactics, Today shows them right under "Who's on": the time block and room, each session with presenters and topics, mock-oral slots, the faculty member's call button, "Live now" / "In 20 min", what it's about, and Join Zoom, Dial in and the meeting ID and passcode to copy. Residents see their own presentations and mock-oral slots on their profile, with a heads-up when they're on vacation, post-call, coming off nights or at another site that day. Sessions go into the calendar feed with the Zoom link (mock orals only for the residents examined); each resident can leave didactics out of their feed.
- **Your real hours**: under More › Duty hours, choose the file from your New Innovations View Hours page, or paste the page. On an iPhone: sign in in Safari, tap Full Site, go to Work Hours › View Hours, tap Export To Excel, then Download, and pick the GridExport file in the app. The app compares your logged hours with the schedule by type of day, shows your real 4-week average and hours by rotation, and predicts the weeks ahead from your own averages. No password is involved. The import also notes that real hours are useful information for the chiefs. The hours are kept on the device, with a copy on the server for Admin › Fairness › Hours; other residents can't read them. Only the device that first sent them can update them (a new phone can take them over, and admins see that they moved), and Remove deletes both copies.
- **Reminders**: until you've added your hours, rated the shifts and turned on alerts, Today shows one reminder at a time, and its button opens the task itself. "Later" hides it for three days.
- **Add to Home Screen**: opened in a phone's browser, Today first shows how to put the app on the Home Screen, ahead of any other reminder. On iPhone that's the Share steps (it's the only way an iPhone allows web alerts); in Chrome on Android it's an Install button. It goes away once the app is opened from the Home Screen, or when "Hide" is tapped.
- **Requests** (the card at the top of More): three things a resident asks of the schedule, each ending in an email draft to dmc.ane.sch@gmail.com, opened in the mail app ready to send (or copied).
  - **Sick call**: today or tomorrow, what you were on, the Jeopardy resident with a text already written (plus classmates who look free for a call shift), and the email.
  - **Swap**: post one of your upcoming call shifts (late days, 24s, 12s, a night-float week, 10–7). Your class sees it under More › Swaps, and Today points out a request you look free for. A classmate offers to take it or to trade one of theirs; you accept, and the app writes the email for either of you to send. Only the device that posted a request or made an offer can change it. The call sheet changes only after the email.
  - **No call**: once a month, pick the weekend days (Friday to Sunday) you'd prefer not to have call next month, choose a reason for each (wedding, family event, travel, conference or exam, religious observance, medical appointment, childcare, personal/wellness, other), add a note, and send the email. The picks are remembered on the device.
- **Requests reach the chiefs in the app too** (Admin › Requests), on top of the emails. A sick call is logged the moment the resident texts, calls or emails from the sick-call sheet; a swap when both residents agree; a no-call list when it's emailed (a re-sent list replaces the old one). The inbox puts sick calls first, then swaps to put on the call sheet, then each month's no-call lists gathered by day (who'd rather not have call, and why). "Done" and "Mark as read" are shared by every admin, and items clear themselves once their dates pass (no-call lists when the month starts). A swap undone after it was marked done comes back flagged. Admins see a red dot on More for a sick call today or tomorrow and a blue one for anything else waiting, plus a card on Today for a sick call or a swap in the next week. On an admin's phone with alerts on, **Smart** (the default) sends a sick call right away and one summary of new swaps and no-call lists around 6–7p, only on days something new came in; **Sick calls** sends only those; **Off** sends nothing. The summary is a Netlify scheduled function (`requests-digest`, 23:00 UTC).
- **Live daily sheet** (Admin › Uploads): connect the board runner's Google workbook once. The app reads it every 3 minutes from noon to 10p, while the sheet is made and posted, and every 15 minutes from about 5a to noon and 10p to midnight (a Netlify scheduled function, `daily-sync`, that runs every 3 minutes and returns at once when no read is due: each read costs Netlify credits), and also whenever someone opens the app and nobody has read it in the last minute (at most one extra read a minute, however many open it; no extra function runs, since it rides on the app's own data request). Any change shows in the app within minutes, marked "Still being finalized" for a future day. The alert goes out as soon as the sheet is ready: after 2p and before 10p, once it has sat unchanged for 10 minutes with every room staffed and the egress tab on the same date (30 minutes if at least 80% of rooms are staffed). Opening the app never sends it; only the scheduled check does. After that, the app shows "OR sheet posted 4:12p" (with "· updated 4:31p" if it changed since), and a correction reaches only the residents whose own assignment changed, 10 minutes after the edits stop, up to three times each per day. Versions that look like the sheet being cleared or yesterday's rooms under a new date are held back, and the admin card says why. If the sheet can't be read for 30 minutes between noon and 10p, admin phones get one alert ("The OR sheet isn't coming in", with the reason), at most once every 6 hours. The admin card shows the newest sheet in the app however it got there (this sheet, a photo, typed in), so it never looks stuck on an old read.
  - **Which day it's for**: the date at the top isn't always changed when the board runner starts the next day's sheet, and sometimes it's cleared altogether; neither stops the sheet from coming in, and a stale date never skips a day (`sheetDay` in `lib/daily-sync.js`). A date ahead at the top is used as is; otherwise a date ahead on the Egress tab; otherwise the rooms decide: mostly today's saved rooms are today's sheet being touched up, mostly the next OR day's are that day's, and new rooms are the next OR day's (weekends and holidays skipped), except on a morning with no sheet for today yet. The admin card says "Filed under Mon 11/30" and why.
  - **Everything else on the workbook**: the board runner's own list beside the table (Resident, Location that day, Late/Call), the Egress tab's residents and its attendings by slot ("H1 (MOR call)", "OB 14 (OB call)", "CV (CHR & CSG)", pre- and post-call, the board runner, OPSC, Meyers), procedure rows below the table with their MDA, the resident sent there (Resident/CRNA column) and their JA, and the notes. A resident placed somewhere the call sheet doesn't say (the cath lab, NORA) gets it as their day, with the MDA's call button; a note that names them shows on their day; Tonight lists the attendings on call and where every MDA is, and Today's "Who's on" shows the MDAs on call. The board runner (the Egress tab's "Board Runner" MDA, and the resident marked "BR" beside them) has a card with both board runner phones at the top of the Board and of Tonight, on Today on an OR or call day (a card in Arrange), in "Who's on", in the OR sheet's details and in Quick call. A day whose sheet came from a photo has no Egress tab, so no name.
  - The link is kept on the server only: not in the code, the dataset or backups. The WhatsApp photo upload still works as a backup, and saving a sheet there counts as announcing it.
- **Lock-screen alerts**: More › Alerts. When a day's OR sheet settles on the live sheet, or an admin saves it for the first time (today or later), everyone who turned alerts on gets a notification such as "Tomorrow's OR schedule is posted. You're in OR 7 with Smith." Tapping it opens that day. A later fix only alerts again if the admin ticks the box. It skips people the sheet doesn't concern that day (off, on vacation, post-call, on night float, or at a hospital the Harper sheet doesn't cover), unless the sheet names them, and the admin card counts them as skipped rather than missed. On iPhone this works from the Home Screen app (iOS 16.4 or later). The server makes its own push keys on first use and keeps them in Blobs; set `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` (and optionally `VAPID_SUBJECT`) to use your own. It only sends to Apple, Google, Mozilla and Microsoft push services. Alerts are written in Apple's declarative format (iOS 18.4 and later), so the iPhone shows them by itself even when the app's background script isn't woken in time; older iPhones and other browsers read the same message in `sw.js`.
- **Didactics alerts**, worked out from each resident's own day (Netlify scheduled function `didactic-alerts`, every 5 minutes; each alert goes out once):
  - **Everyone a session is for**: one heads-up per sitting (back-to-back sessions in one room count as one). 10 minutes before an in-person session, 5 before one that's only on Zoom, and 5 before with the link for someone at another hospital. Nothing for someone off, on vacation, post-call and gone home, on night float that night, or still on call, the late shift or the 10a–7p at the time (from 3p on). Coming off night float or 24-hr call at 6a still counts as in the building, so they hear about the 6a conference. Each phone can turn these off in More › Alerts.
  - **Presenting**: at 7p the evening before, the time, place, moderator and a nudge to have the slides on hand; then 30 minutes before the sitting starts (20 on Zoom), saying when to be in the room to load slides. No "starts in 5 minutes".
  - **Mock oral**: at 7p the evening before, with the examiner; then 15 minutes before their own slot.
  - Presenting and mock-oral alerts aren't affected by the switch, and nothing goes to someone on vacation that day.
- **Case log for ACGME** (More › Case log, and a card on Today after a case day). ACGME takes no imports, so the app does everything up to ACGME's own Submit button:
  - **Laid out from the OR sheet**: one card per case on the sheet, each with its Case ID in the resident's own style (11/18/26-Surgeon-1 until they pick another), the date, site and supervisor (the sheet's MDA, matched to the directory). The first case starts from what the resident did last time with that surgeon; later cases in the room copy the case before them until changed. So a room of the same operation is one preset plus an ASA tap per case.
  - **Looks like ACGME's own Add Case page**: one case is open at a time, top to bottom in ACGME's order and words (Case Date, Site, Supervisor, Patient Age "a. < 3 months"…"e. >= 65 year", Life-Threatening Pathology, Difficult Airway Management, Add Comments, Group "Procedures / Pain Consultations and Procedures", ASA Physical Status in its Non-emergency and Emergency rows, then every section's boxes under ACGME's sub-headings, in the page's order: `pageLayout` in `public/core/acgme.js`, read off the real page). Square boxes for ACGME's checkboxes, dots for its one-of choices. The app's Quick fill sits just above the boxes it ticks, the optional site lists open by themselves for a spinal or a block, and the other cases fold to one line (supervisor, age, ASA, what was done). "Next case" moves down the day.
  - **A different supervisor per case**: each case has its own Supervisor field; the picker lists the day's sheet MDAs first (both names of an "A / B" room), then recent ones, and from a case can also set the cases after it ("the attending changed"). The day's header sets the whole day and says "2 supervisors" when they differ.
  - **Deleting what won't be entered**: "Delete case" in any open case (a sheet case never filled in says "Not a case" and leaves the day), and Case log › "N cases saved, not in ACGME yet · See or delete" lists every waiting case from every day, to open one or delete it, or delete all the incomplete ones at once. Deletions reach the server like any change; nothing in ACGME is touched.
  - **Case IDs, each resident's own way** (Case log › Case IDs › Change, or tap any Case ID): ready-made styles, each shown with the resident's own next cases (date-surgeon-number, one running number, date and running number, date and case of the day, surgeon-number, date-room-number, or blank, which ACGME allows), or their own from parts in any order (date in seven formats, surgeon or procedure, room, hospital, initials, their own text, case # of the day, case # with that surgeon, running #) with a choice of separator. A running number starts wherever they say ("Next case is #"); the Log cases button reads ACGME's "Cases Submitted" count each time it runs, so the sheet offers "Match ACGME", and the Case log offers a one-tap catch-up when ACGME's count gets ahead. Numbers go to cases not yet in ACGME oldest first, the order the button enters them, so they track ACGME's own count; cases already in ACGME never change, and the server keeps the highest number used so none is handed out twice. Any single case can be renamed by hand or switched to another style with one tap, and "Back to your style" undoes it. Logic in `public/core/acgme.js` (`planIds`, `formatCaseId`).
  - **Any combination**: every box toggles on its own (nothing ticks or unticks another), and presets add their boxes rather than replace, so two make a combination; tapping a highlighted preset takes its boxes off, apart from ones another highlighted preset uses. Only ACGME's own single choices stay single: age, life-threatening pathology, difficult airway, and one ASA class (with E).
  - **Every option on ACGME's form** (both groups: anesthetics, and pain consults and procedures), with one-tap presets for the kind of day (OB, cardiac, thoracic, vascular, neuro, peds, regional, pain clinic, NORA, call), quick extras (art line, central line, blocks, high-risk, trauma), "Same again", and after-midnight dates for call. A resident's own frequent combinations show first. Nothing about the patient beyond ACGME's age group and ASA.
  - **Saved on the phone at once**, then on the server (`cases/<pid>` in Blobs), readable only by the devices the resident uses (a hash of each device key; another device can join after a confirm). Entered cases are kept a week, then folded into a short summary of habits. Cases not yet in ACGME are never dropped.
  - **A procedure on its own** (an intern's art line, a central line or intubation on an ICU month): "Log a procedure" on the Case log, and a one-line shortcut on Today for interns and ICU months (Art line, Central line, Ultrasound-guided IV, Intubation; PA catheter and nerve block one screen away). One tap adds it with its Case ID (11/18/26-Aline-1); the supervisor from one logged in the past week carries over, so it's often ready at once. No ASA needed. It's a shortcut, not a reminder: interns get no daily prompts or nudges, only the Sunday one if something they logged is still waiting for ACGME.
  - **Into ACGME**: a "Log cases" bookmark (set up once: a Safari favorite on iPhone, the bookmarks bar on a computer, or an emailed link for the computer). From the Home Screen app, ACGME and the setup page open in Safari itself (an `x-safari-https://` address, iOS 17 and later; checked on the simulator), where the favorite and saved passwords are; older iPhones get a sheet explaining the in-app browser's Safari button. On iPhone it runs from Bookmarks (address bar › book icon › Log cases): Safari refuses to run it from an address-bar suggestion. The bookmark loads `public/acgme.js` on ACGME's Add Case page through the resident's private link (`/api/acgme/<token>`, CORS for apps.acgme.org only). Each tap fills the next ready case: Case ID, date, site, supervisor, age, ASA, every box, case types, comments. It never submits. ACGME sends phones to a different Add Case page (`CaseEntryMobile`, a few steps with Next, codes loaded from ACGME's server); there the button fills the details, adds each code through ACGME's own add (the same as tapping it), and stops on the last step with Submit at the bottom. When Submit is pressed, or the case appears in ACGME's Last 5 Cases, it's marked entered in the app; a case it can't find there gets a question rather than a second fill.
  - **Nudges** (More › Alerts › Case log nudges, on by default, only for someone who has started a case log; scheduled function `caselog-nudges`, every 15 minutes): 15 minutes after a case day's shift ends if nothing (or not everything) is logged (not for interns), 7:30 the next morning after an overnight shift, and Sunday at 7p when ready cases have waited five days or more. "I had no cases this day" silences a day.
- **Program emails** (Admin › Email intake): a Google Apps Script, copied from that card into the Gmail account that gets the program's emails, checks every 30 minutes and sends the program coordinators' emails that went to 10 or more people to the app's private address (`/api/mail/<token>`, `lib/mail-intake.js`). Personal emails (to a resident or two) never leave Gmail, and the server skips any that do without keeping their subject. The coordinators' addresses aren't in this public code: the script and the server compare salted hashes (`MAIL_SENDERS`, comma-separated addresses, adds more). What's in an email goes where it belongs:
  - **The monthly call schedule** (`.xlsx` or `.xlsm`): merged like an upload, for this month or later only, never over a newer copy (a later email or an admin's upload), and a draft ("1st DRAFT of DECEMBER…") is marked as one until the final arrives. The month view says so.
  - **The conference schedule PDF**: read by Claude in the background (`read-mail-background.mjs`) and saved for its month, unless an admin already saved that month by hand.
  - **Calendar invites** (`.ics`): events as written. **Any other email**: read by Claude for events with a date (a party, grand rounds, a town hall, a simulation session, an RSVP deadline), who they're for, cancellations and moved dates (`lib/mail-read.js`). A follow-up that names the event differently updates it rather than adding a second one.
  - Events show under More › Events, on Today (today, tomorrow, and RSVPs due within three days), in "Coming up" and "Looking ahead", and in each resident's calendar feed. One for a class ("CA-1s") shows only for that class outside the Events page.
- **Call**: one-tap calling for residents, attendings (mobile and Cisco), OR rooms, units and service lines. Open it from the phone button at the top of every screen, or from More. Anesthesia techs change rooms daily, so every OR card has a Tech button that opens the whole list, with the tech you called that day on top. "Right now" lists who you're likely to need today (your room, your MDA, your JA, the board runners). Every OR card in the app has call chips for the room and its MDA. Pagers and hospital-only extensions copy to the clipboard.

It installs to the home screen ("Add to Home Screen") and works offline with the last-loaded schedule.

## Privacy

No schedule data lives in this repository. Uploads are stored in Netlify Blobs and served only to people with the passcode. Calendar feeds use a per-resident secret link. Spreadsheets, photos and backups are git-ignored.

## Deploy on Netlify (one time)

1. In Netlify: **Add new site → Import an existing project → GitHub →** pick this repository. The settings come from `netlify.toml` (no build command, publish `public`). Deploy.
2. **Site configuration → Environment variables**, add:

   | Variable | What it's for |
   |---|---|
   | `APP_PASSCODE` | Shared passcode residents type once on their phone. |
   | `ADMIN_KEY` | Long random string for uploads and edits; keep it to yourself. Generate one with `openssl rand -base64 24`. |
   | `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` | Optional. Turns on reading the daily photo, the monthly didactics PDF and the program's emails with Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`), roughly 5–15¢ each. Get it at console.anthropic.com and set a monthly spend limit. |
   | `CAL_SECRET` | Optional. Signs calendar links and the Email intake address (defaults to `ADMIN_KEY`). Changing it breaks everyone's existing calendar links and the Gmail script's address (copy it again from Admin › Email intake). |
   | `MAIL_SENDERS` | Optional. More addresses, comma-separated, whose emails to the program Email intake accepts (beyond the two coordinators). |

3. **Deploys → Trigger deploy** so the functions pick up the variables.
4. Open the site, enter the passcode, then **More → Admin sign-in** with the admin key. Upload the annual rotations file and the monthly call file.

## Every day / every month

- **Daily**: the sheet is posted the evening before. Save the WhatsApp photo, then **Admin → Daily OR sheet → Read a photo**. You don't pick a date: the app files the sheet under the date printed on it, or tomorrow if no date can be read, and you can change it in the review screen. Check what Claude read and **Save**. Without an API key, use **Enter by hand**.
  - Resident names are always saved in the reference-list (roster) spelling: misspellings and initials are fixed on save, and the app tells you what it changed.
  - A role written in a name cell, such as "PAT" in the Breaks column, becomes that day's PAT resident: the name in the sheet's PAT box, or else whoever the call sheet has on PAT (including someone covering PAT). "AP" becomes the most senior resident on Acute Pain who isn't on a call shift that day.
  - Once tomorrow's sheet is in, everyone's Today screen shows a "Tomorrow" card with their room, start time and MDA.
- **Monthly**: the call schedule comes in by itself from the coordinator's email once Email intake is set up. Otherwise upload the `.xlsx` or `.xlsm` when it's emailed. Re-uploading a month replaces it.
- **Monthly didactics**: **Admin → Didactics → Read a PDF** with the conference schedule. Claude reads the sessions, presenters, mock-oral slots and Zoom details (with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`); review and **Save**. Resident names are saved in roster spelling. Without the key the PDF is still attached and you can enter sessions by hand. In the editor, people are one per line: `Name | role | topic | 06:00-06:30` (roles: presenter, examinee, moderator, faculty, examiner).
- **Phone directory**: **Admin → Directory → Import .txt**, review, **Save**. Keep the file private; it is never part of this repository. Format:

  ```
  ## Dialing
  Ext 5xxxx = 313-745-xxxx
  ## Residents
  Last, First | Cisco 313-555-0100 | Pager 1234
  ## Resident mobiles
  Last, First | 248-555-0103
  ## Anesthesiologists — Harper
  Last, First | Cisco 313-555-0101 | Mobile 248-555-0102
  ## Harper OR rooms
  OR 15 | Ext 51234
  ```

  Label each number with what it is: **Cisco** (hospital Cisco phone), **Mobile** (personal cell; "Cell" also works), **Pager**, **Ext** or **Vocera**. In a section whose title says "mobile" or "Cisco", unlabeled numbers take that label, and listing someone again adds the numbers to what they already have. The app calls the mobile by default, then Cisco, and mobiles also get a text button. Calendar feeds show the work phone where there is one, so personal numbers stay off outside calendar services.

  "Residents" match the roster (a new name with "| PGY-1" is added), "Anesthesiologists …" sections are matched to the MDAs on the daily sheet (the site in the title breaks ties, e.g. Harper vs. Sinai-Grace), "Harper OR rooms" and "OPSC rooms" power the room buttons, and "Dialing" rules turn 5-digit extensions into numbers a cell phone can call.
- **Someone leaves the program**: **Admin → People → ✕** removes them (roster, call-sheet rows, rotations, phone numbers). Saving the directory also drops anyone who is no longer listed and has no schedule anywhere.
- **Past months**: upload older call sheets any time to fill in the fairness history, plus that year's rotations file, which says who was in each class that year (so visiting rotators aren't scored). Anyone on an older sheet but not the newest one stays in the history and off the roster.
- **Yearly**: upload the new rotations file in June.

## How the call sheet is read

| On the sheet | In the app |
|---|---|
| `H1` / blue Friday fill | 24-hour call, 6a–6a, Friday and Saturday only (covers that night) |
| `H2` | 12-hour call, 6a–6p, Saturday and Sunday only |
| Green fill | Late resident: finish at your site, then Harper until 6p |
| `NF` | Night float, 6p–6a, Sunday to Thursday nights |
| `10-7` (Excel shows it as a date) | CA-3 senior 10a–7p |
| `11-9`, or a CA-1 on Obstetrics / `OB` | The "11 to 9" rotation: CA-1 OB at Hutzel, Monday to Friday 11a–9p |
| Magenta week | Cardiac call (home call, not counted in hours) |
| `Vac` / peach block | Vacation; a peach day for everyone is a holiday (CRNA coverage) |
| `PC` | Post-call day off |
| Bold / red text | Shown as "Covering" / "Changed" |
| `X` in an OR | CRNA room |

Regular-day hours (default 6a–5p) and the staffing rules behind the gap checks are editable in **Admin → Settings**.

## Local development

```bash
npm install
npm run dev     # http://localhost:8888 with passcode "dev" and admin key "devadmin"
npm test
npm run icons   # regenerate the app icons
```

`npm run dev` serves `public/` and runs the same functions as Netlify, storing data in `.localdata/`.

## Layout

- `public/`: the app (no build step). `public/core/` holds the schedule logic shared with the server: day resolution, hours, gaps, swaps and the iCalendar feed.
- `lib/`: server-only code: the `.xlsx` reader, parsers, storage, auth and the photo reader.
- `netlify/functions/`: `api.mjs` (all API routes and `/cal/*.ics` feeds), `parse-photo-background.mjs`, `parse-didactics-background.mjs` and `read-mail-background.mjs` (photo, PDF and email reading, up to 15 minutes each), `daily-sync.mjs` (reads the live sheet every 3 minutes while it's made, every 15 otherwise) and `requests-digest.mjs` (the chiefs' evening summary).
- Shared records (the dataset, swaps, requests, ratings, alert subscriptions) change only through `st.update` or `withDataset`, so two saves at the same moment can't overwrite each other. `test/structure.test.mjs` enforces this, and checks that every import resolves and every module is in the offline list.
