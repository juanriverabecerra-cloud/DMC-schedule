Your Log cases button

It fills ACGME's Add Case page from your DMC Schedule case log, one case per tap. You check each case and tap Submit.

  1. Copy the button.
    Copied
  2. Add this page to Favorites. Tap ≡ beside the address › Tab Actions › Add to Favorites (or Share › Add to Favorites), then Save. It's already named Log cases.
  3. Make it the button. Tap the address bar, then the book icon at the top. Touch and hold Log cases › Edit. Clear the address, paste, and tap Save.
  4. Use it. Open ACGME
    Sign in (Safari fills in your password). Then tap the address bar, the book icon if Bookmarks aren't showing, and Log cases. Each tap fills the next case.
    Typing its name in the address bar won't work: Safari only runs it from Bookmarks.

Log cases

  1. Show the bookmarks bar if it's hidden: Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  2. Drag the blue button onto it.
  3. On ACGME's Add Case page, click Log cases: it fills your next case. Check it and click Submit. Click Log cases again for the next.
